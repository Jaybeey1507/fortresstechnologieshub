Canadian Data Sovereignty

Your core data
stays in Canada.

Most AI assistants are run by foreign companies, which can place your conversations under foreign laws no matter where the data is stored.

We made a different choice. CoreMind's language models, conversations, and memory run on Canadian-owned infrastructure located in Canada, operated by a Canadian company and governed by Canadian law.

01

Canadian-owned infrastructure

CoreMind's core runs on Canadian-owned servers located in Canada, chosen specifically so your data stays under Canadian jurisdiction.

02

Our own models

Our core language models, embeddings, and voice processing run on our own servers. Your everyday conversations are not sent to outside AI providers.

03

Built for regulated work

Designed with Canadian privacy standards in mind for healthcare, legal, financial, and public sector teams that need AI without cross-border exposure.

04

Clear about the exceptions

A few optional features, such as live web search, image generation, and video generation, rely on trusted external services. They are clearly disclosed in our Privacy Policy, and you choose when to use them.
