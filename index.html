Canadian Data Sovereignty

Your Data
Stays in
Canada.

Most AI companies are American. That means your data, your conversations, and your business information are subject to the US CLOUD Act — which allows the US government to compel American companies to hand over data stored anywhere in the world.

At Fortress Technologies Hub, we made a different choice. CoreMind runs entirely on Canadian-owned infrastructure in Montreal, Quebec. Your data never crosses the border.

01

Canadian-Owned Infrastructure

CoreMind runs on Patrii Cloud, a Canadian-owned server provider in Montreal, Quebec. Not AWS. Not Google Cloud. Not Azure.

02

No CLOUD Act Exposure

As a Canadian company on Canadian infrastructure, your data is not subject to US government data requests under the CLOUD Act or other foreign surveillance legislation.

03

Self-Hosted AI Models

Our core language models, embeddings, and voice processing all run on our own servers. Your conversations are not sent to American AI providers.

04

Built for Canadian Businesses

Designed with Canadian privacy standards in mind, giving healthcare, legal, financial, and government organizations true compliance confidence.
