Introducing Fortress Technologies Hub

We are a Canadian company building privacy-first AI and enterprise API security. Here is who we are, what we believe, and what we are building.

JA
Jubril Akanbi, Founder and CEO
November 2, 2026 · 2 min read

Fortress Technologies Hub is a federally incorporated Canadian technology company based in Toronto. We build two products: CoreMind, a privacy-first AI assistant, and API Fortress, AI-powered security for the APIs that modern businesses run on.

Both products come from the same belief: powerful technology and trustworthy technology should not be a trade-off. You should not have to give up your privacy to use a great AI assistant, and you should not need a ten-person security team to protect your APIs.

What we believe

A lot of technology is built to grow first and earn trust later. We are doing it the other way around. Privacy and security are designed into our products from the first line of code, not added after a problem shows up.

  • Privacy first. Your data belongs to you. We build systems that protect it, not systems that harvest it.
  • Human centered. Technology should make people more capable, not replace their judgment.
  • Honest by default. We say clearly what our products do, where your data goes, and where the exceptions are.
  • Built in Canada. We are a Canadian company, building on Canadian infrastructure, under Canadian law.

CoreMind: AI that thinks with you, not about you

CoreMind is an AI assistant for writing, research, analysis, and hard problems. It offers three models, from fast everyday help to deep step-by-step reasoning, along with voice conversations, file and image understanding, memory, and a developer API.

What sets it apart is where it runs. CoreMind's own language models, your conversations, and your memory run on Canadian-owned infrastructure located in Canada. Read more about CoreMind and data sovereignty.

API Fortress: security that explains itself

APIs connect almost everything today: mobile apps, payments, health records, and internal tools. They are also one of the most targeted parts of modern software. API Fortress sits in front of your services, inspects every request, and blocks attacks in real time. When it stops something, its AI Security Assistant tells your team what happened and how to fix the root cause. Read more about API Fortress.

Research at CoreMind Labs

We also run CoreMind Labs, our research group focused on AI that is safe, capable, and aligned with what people actually mean. Our first paper, on intent-aware reasoning in large language models, is expected in December 2026. Read about the paper.

Why we started this blog

This is where we will share product updates, research, security insights, and the thinking behind what we build. To follow along, bookmark this page or follow us on LinkedIn.

Innovation is not about replacing human potential. It is about unlocking it.Jubril Akanbi, Founder and CEO
