CoreMind: A Privacy-First AI Assistant, Built in Canada

Powerful AI should not require giving up control of your data. Here is what CoreMind does, and exactly what data sovereignty means in practice.

JA
Jubril Akanbi, Founder and CEO
November 2, 2026 · 2 min read

People tell AI assistants things they would not post anywhere else: contract details, health questions, business plans, code from private projects, drafts they are not ready to share. That makes one question more important than most people realize: where do those conversations actually go?

CoreMind was built to give a clear answer.

What CoreMind can do

CoreMind is a full AI assistant for thinking, writing, research, and problem solving. A few examples of everyday use:

  • Read the fine print. Upload an agreement and CoreMind flags the clauses worth a closer look, such as auto-renewals, liability caps, and data ownership terms.
  • Explain what you are looking at. Share a screenshot of an error or a photo of a document and get a plain-language explanation.
  • Think a decision through. Reasoning mode works through hard problems step by step and shows its work, so you can check it.
  • Talk instead of type. Have natural voice conversations, with answers spoken back to you.
  • Pick up where you left off. Memory and context mean CoreMind remembers how you like things done.
  • Turn ideas into documents. Go from a rough idea to a structured plan, report, or draft.

Three models for different kinds of work

  • CM-1 is fast and efficient, for quick questions, brainstorming, and drafting.
  • CM-1o balances speed and depth, for detailed analysis, complex writing, and code.
  • CM-o1 is our deep reasoning model, for multi-step problems that need careful, methodical thinking.

Developers can use all three through the CoreMind API, with streaming, file and image uploads, and usage analytics built in.

What data sovereignty means in practice

Data sovereignty means your data is governed by the laws of the place where it lives and the company that handles it. When your conversations are processed by a company based in another country, they can fall under that country's laws, no matter where the servers sit.

CoreMind is built differently:

  • Canadian-owned infrastructure. CoreMind's core runs on Canadian-owned servers located in Canada.
  • Our own models. Our core language models, embeddings, and voice processing run on our own servers, so your everyday conversations are not sent to outside AI providers.
  • Trained in Canada. Our model training also happens on Canadian infrastructure.
  • Canadian law. CoreMind is built and operated by a Canadian company, under Canadian privacy law.
Clear about the exceptionsA few optional features, such as live web search, image generation, and video generation, rely on trusted external services. These are disclosed in our Privacy Policy, and you choose when to use them. Being specific about this is part of earning your trust.

Who CoreMind is for

CoreMind works for anyone who wants a capable AI assistant without the privacy trade-off. It is especially relevant for Canadian professionals and organizations in healthcare, law, finance, and the public sector, where sending sensitive information across borders is a real concern.
